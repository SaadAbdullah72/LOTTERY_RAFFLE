# Report: Competition Participation and Achievements

## 1. Event Overview

* **Event Title:** #BuildOnAlgo - Ideathon
* **Organizing Body:** Algorand Pakistan / Algorand Foundation
* **Date:** October 24, 2025
* **Time:** 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM
* **Venue:** National Incubation Center (NIC), Karachi

---

## 2. Participation & Selection Details

This competition recognized the **Top 12 Projects** on the Algorand Blockchain across Pakistan. Participation included selection for two distinct startup projects:

### Project A: Trust-Vault3 (formerly Guardian Angel)

* **Participation Type:** Team Member
* **Role:** Co-Founder
* **Recognition:** Selected in the **Top 12 Teams** nationwide.

### Project B: Agrochain

* **Participation Type:** Individual / Team Lead
* **Role:** Founder
* **Recognition:** Selected as one of the **Top 12 Teams** nationwide to pitch at NIC Karachi.

---

## 3. International Engagement

* **Event:** Algorand International Pitch Competition.
* **Qualification Criteria:** Eligibility was restricted to individuals holding an **Algorand Blockchain Certified Masterclass Certificate**.
* **Achievement:** Successfully participated in the international segment of the pitch competition representing Pakistan-based innovation.
